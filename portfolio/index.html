Telecom Customer 360Overview

Turned 50 million log records into two Power BI views for a content team. They showed that 71.64% of contracts watched on 15 or more days in April 2022, and that 69.13% of returning users changed their top search category.

Context
A TV and streaming service wanted to know how often customers watch, what they spend their time on, and whether their search interests shift between periods. Viewing logs identify subscriptions by contract, while search logs identify people by user ID, so each question needed its own dataset.
My role
Solo project: pipeline, metric definitions and dashboard.
Approach
  • Kept viewing at contract level and search at user level, so no metric mixes the two kinds of identity.
  • Defined activity as distinct viewing days, with 15 or more in April counting as highly active, and grouped app codes into five content groups.
  • Ranked each user’s keywords with Spark SQL’s ROW_NUMBER, then sent only the distinct top keywords to GPT-4o-mini in batches for genre labels.
  • Loaded the contract-level and user-level tables into MySQL over JDBC for Power BI.
Result
TV led watch time for 97.15% of all contracts. Among the 86,172 users who searched in both 1–14 June and 1–14 July 2022, 59,570 (69.13%) changed their top category. I suggested prioritizing TV in a homepage content review and testing C Drama suggestions for users moving from Drama.
Limits
When two keywords tie for a user’s top rank, the pipeline does not break the tie.

01Recruitment Data Platform

Hourly job-ad metrics from a stream of recruitment events.

May 2026Personal project

Download PDFPDF Source codeCode
Recruitment Data PlatformOverview
Next projectRetail Banking Lakehouse

Built a Kafka and Spark pipeline that turns raw job-ad events into six hourly KPIs per ad and channel, processing only new events every minute instead of recounting the whole log.

Context
A job ad can run on several channels at once. To compare channels within the same hour, a recruitment team had to group raw clicks and applications by ad, channel and hour for every report. The project automates that table, using simulated events.
My role
Solo project.
Approach
  • Parsed Kafka JSON against an explicit schema in Spark Structured Streaming and checkpointed progress, so a restarted stream resumes where it stopped.
  • Read only events newer than a high-water mark stored in MySQL, so each run processes new data instead of rescanning the whole log.
  • Defined six KPIs per hour, job, publisher, campaign and group: clicks, conversions, qualified and disqualified applications, hourly spend and average bid. A join to the job table adds company IDs.
  • Scheduled the batch every minute in Airflow and ran the stack with Docker Compose.
Result
The pipeline produces an hourly table that can be filtered by job, channel, campaign and group. I verified the KPI formulas by recomputing 20 logged events from one group and hour: 10 clicks, 30.00 spend and a 1.50 average bid.
Limits
Results are appended, so an hour whose events arrive in two batches gets two rows and needs a final sum when reported. All events are simulated.

02Retail Banking Customer 360 Lakehouse

Historized customer dimensions, validated marts and masked reporting views.

Jul 2026Course projectTop 3 in cohort

Download PDFPDF Source codeCode
Retail Banking LakehouseOverview
Previous projectRecruitment Data Platform

Built a Customer 360 lakehouse that gives marketing one consistent, masked row per customer per day for 10,000 simulated banking customers. It was recognized as a Top 3 project in the COLE banking data engineering course.

Context
In the simulated bank, customer data sits in core banking (Oracle) and in card and CRM systems (PostgreSQL), with different keys and levels of detail. Marketing needs one customer view, a history of changes and no access to raw personal data. IT wants fewer reports querying the source systems directly.
My role
The course provided a starting demo with ingestion and basic analysis. I added dimension history, safe reruns, orchestration, quality checks and the masked serving layer.
Approach
  • Kept history for customer, account, product and branch with SCD Type 2, and joined facts to the version valid at the time, so past reports show customers as they were. Rerunning a batch creates no duplicate versions.
  • Aggregated balances, loans and transactions per customer before joining, so the Gold mart keeps one row per customer and date instead of multiplying rows.
  • Ran eight child DAGs from one daily parent DAG that passes the business date and run ID, and logged 19 quality checks per run to PostgreSQL. A failed critical check fails the run.
  • Masked personal data and limited the marketing role to the sandbox through Trino.
Result
In the acceptance run for business date 2026-01-01, all eight child DAGs succeeded and 19 of 19 checks passed. The current mart holds 10,000 unique customers and the history mart 20,000 snapshot rows across two dates.
Limits
All data is synthetic, and the acceptance run was triggered manually rather than on the daily schedule.

Fashion E-commerceOverview

In a thesis graded 9.1/10, built admin analytics that show which searches fail and whether recommendations lead to purchases, along with the mobile app’s recommender and virtual try-on.

Context
Store staff need to see which searches return nothing and where shoppers drop off after seeing a recommendation. Shoppers need suggestions that fit what they are looking at, and a way to try clothes on before buying.
My role
Team of two. I owned the React Native app, the recommender, virtual try-on and the admin analytics. My teammate built the customer web app and image search.
Approach
  • Defined the zero-result rate as the share of searches in a period that return no products, and listed the failing queries so staff can add synonyms or categories.
  • Logged recommendation impressions, clicks, add-to-cart events, orders and payments, then reported CTR, step conversion and net attributed revenue by placement and algorithm version.
  • Ranked recommendations by context: shopper behavior on Home, item attributes for similar products and outfit compatibility in the Cart.
Result
Staff can compare placements and algorithm versions and see where the funnel drops. In an offline benchmark on 100 Amazon Fashion cases, the Home recommender scored 0.0615 NDCG@5 against 0.0063 for a most-popular baseline.
Limits
Dashboard figures come from demo usage, not a live store.

01Hotel Digital Transformation

Occupancy analysis, a pricing proposal and an operations app with embedded reporting.

2026, eight weeksClient project

Download PDFPDF Demo (sample data)Demo
Hotel Digital TransformationOverview
Next projectKalotoys WMS

Showed why a hotel branch missed its revenue target: occupancy fell to 17.83% in October, while staff felt on target because they took 7 in 10 bookings. Then helped replace its paper bookings with an app and checked Power BI reports.

Context
A hotel branch in Ho Chi Minh City was missing its revenue KPI while staff believed they had hit their targets. Bookings, stays and payments were written on paper, so nobody could check where the gap came from.
My role
Intern on a SonDoong Labs client team. I reconciled the records and ran the analysis, built booking features in the API, and set up the reporting sync.
Approach
  • Standardized booking codes, stay dates and channels across paper logs, Excel files and payment records, so bookings could be compared by room type and channel.
  • Compared room nights, occupancy, room types and channels in Excel PivotTables for June to October 2024.
  • Built booking features in an ASP.NET Core API on Azure App Service with Azure SQL, so each booking and payment is recorded once.
  • Synced the Power BI data to Fabric Warehouse through the REST API, checking row counts with DAX before swapping in the new snapshot in one transaction.
  • Switched the embedded report to Direct Lake, so it never reads a half-loaded snapshot and refreshes when a new version arrives.
Result
Occupancy averaged 46.22% from June to October 2024 and fell to 17.83% in October. Front-desk staff took 70.63% of bookings, which explains why staff felt on target while most rooms stayed empty. Standard and Deluxe rooms brought 67.53% of nights and 66.84% of room revenue. The analysis led to a proposal to raise direct (walk-in and phone) rates by 20% and keep OTA rates unchanged.
Limits
The public demo uses sample data. The hotel’s goal of 20% revenue growth was not measured during the project; that needs actual revenue over a comparable period.

02Kalotoys Warehouse Management System

Stock movements, balance reconciliation and Power BI inventory reporting.

Oct–Nov 2026, four weeksClient project

Download PDFPDF Live demoDemo
Kalotoys WMSOverview
Previous projectHotel Digital Transformation

Gave a toy maker’s warehouse a traceable record of every material movement, so balances reconcile with the ledger and low stock shows up before production runs short.

Context
Kalotoys makes personalized wooden toys. Staff took materials from the warehouse without a consistent record of who took what and when, so the Excel stock count drifted and shortages were noticed only when production needed the material.
My role
Intern on a SonDoong Labs client team. I designed the ledger and balance model, built the movement forms and the reporting, and ran the reconciliation test.
Approach
  • Defined eight movement types in a data dictionary, with one ledger row per material movement at a storage location.
  • Kept the ledger and current balances in separate MongoDB Atlas collections and wrote each movement with its balance update in one transaction, so the two stay consistent.
  • Recorded a transfer as a paired outgoing and incoming row, so total stock does not change when material moves between slots.
  • Checked the available balance before every issue, and tested a daily sync of balances to Fabric Warehouse for Power BI.
Result
Balances recalculated from the ledger matched the stored balances across 660 simulated transactions (713 ledger rows). On the 10 November 2026 sample snapshot, the reports flagged six materials below threshold and seven locations holding stock longer than 45 days, with 28 of 30 slots in use.
Limits
The trial runs on sample data, and the Fabric sync has been tested but is not yet in production.
